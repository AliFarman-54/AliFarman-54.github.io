About

I am a retired Professor of Economics with more than four decades of experience in academic leadership, socioeconomic research, survey design, data analysis, and high-level development consultancy.

I previously served as Director of the Institute of Development Studies (IDS) and the Institute of Business and Management Sciences (IBMS) at the Agricultural University Peshawar, and as Chief Executive of Prudent Consultants. My work has involved close collaboration with international organizations including the Asian Development Bank, IFAD, UNDP, USAID, World Bank, and others.

I hold a Ph.D. in Economics from the University of East Anglia (UK). My doctoral research focused on models of household behaviour in subsistence agriculture. I am now based in Canada and continue to offer research, evaluation, and advisory services.
